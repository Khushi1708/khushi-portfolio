My inspiration
“Technology should be powerful and trustworthy.”

It began with a simple curiosity about how code makes decisions. One Python internship led to robotics, then to websites for real businesses, then to generative and agentic AI. Every build raised the same question: can we trust what a machine tells us? That question took me from building AI apps to researching how to verify them — and it is the thread I want to follow in everything I create.

About me
